Minor Criminal Offences

Minor criminal offence representation in Ontario

Some criminal charges can be handled by a licensed paralegal. Butler Legal Services confirms at the start whether yours is one of them.

Book a Consultation
Home / Services / Minor Criminal Offences
Scope matters here. Paralegals may only represent clients on summary conviction criminal offences where the maximum penalty is six months in jail or less. Whether a charge qualifies depends on the specific offence and how the Crown proceeds, so eligibility is confirmed at the very start. If it does not qualify, that is explained and a lawyer is recommended.

What this service covers

  • Reviewing the charge, the paperwork you were given and the dates you must attend
  • Requesting and reviewing disclosure
  • Explaining your options, the risks and the possible outcomes
  • Speaking with the Crown on your behalf
  • Representation at appearances and at trial

What matters early

  • Any court date on your paperwork is mandatory, so keep every document you were given.
  • Conditions you agreed to, such as an undertaking, must be followed exactly.
  • Share the charge details before the first court date, so options are ready when you attend.

If a lawyer is needed

Serious charges and anything outside a paralegal’s permitted scope need a lawyer. Butler Legal Services will tell you directly rather than accept a retainer it cannot properly act on.
