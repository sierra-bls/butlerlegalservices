Our story

Built on preparation, clarity and getting it right

Handing a ticket, a charge or a claim to someone you have never met is not a small thing. You want to know it is being handled carefully and correctly, by someone who understands what is at stake if it is not.

Butler Legal Services was founded by Sierra Walker, a paralegal licensed by the Law Society of Ontario since 2021. Sierra built her career in corporate governance and finance and at law firms, and has run her sister company, Notable Notary, since 2021 before branching out to open Butler Legal Services. While this practice is new, the experience behind it is not.

That background shapes how the practice works: careful with details, clear about process, and focused on getting it right the first time. Sierra is also the founder of The Notary Blueprint, an education venture that trains other notaries, and regularly speaks and teaches, so explaining complicated rules in plain language is part of the job.

Butler Legal Services represents clients across Ontario, virtually and in person, in Small Claims Court, traffic and highway offence matters, and minor criminal and provincial offence matters. Removing barriers to legal services is central to how the practice works: clear explanations, honest advice and no surprises.

Experience behind the practice

  • Licensed paralegal since April 2021. Licensed and regulated by the Law Society of Ontario.
  • Corporate governance, finance and law firms. A career built on precision, deadlines and working with clients under pressure.
  • Founder of Notable Notary since 2021. A notary, apostille and translation company serving individuals, families and businesses across Ontario, from Ajax, Pickering, Whitby and Oshawa to clients online province-wide.
  • Commissioned Notary Public. Experience with sworn documents, affidavits and statutory declarations, which come up often in court matters.
  • Founder of The Notary Blueprint. An education venture that trains other notaries, with more than 3,000 hours of teaching.
  • Speaker and educator. Speaks and teaches nearly every week, which means explaining complicated rules plainly is part of the job.

How Butler Legal Services works

  • Personalized legal strategy. Each matter gets a plan built around your goals and deadlines.
  • Transparent communication. Plain language and regular updates, with no surprises.
  • Client-focused commitment. Every matter is treated with the care it deserves.
  • Practical solutions. Honest advice on what is worth fighting, with the costs in view.
  • Clear limits. Only matters that can be properly handled within a paralegal’s scope are taken on.
