How pricing works

  • Fees are shown before HST (if applicable) and exclude disbursements such as court fees, disclosure fees, mileage and transcripts.
  • These are starting fees for straightforward matters. Additional trial days, adjournments, appeals and unusually complex files are quoted separately.
  • Not quoted online: fail to remain, collisions with injuries or death, commercial vehicle matters and criminal charges. These need a case review.
  • A written retainer sets out your exact fee before work begins. Advice outside a ticket’s scope is billed at $150 per hour in 6-minute increments.
  • Butler Legal Services does not handle criminal charges. Matters outside a paralegal’s scope are referred to a lawyer.

This tool gives general price information, not legal advice, and no outcome is guaranteed.
