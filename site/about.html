About Butler Legal Services

Practical, transparent legal representation across Ontario, from a licensed paralegal with more than 10 years in legal and client services.

Our story

Sierra Walker

Licensed Paralegal, LSO P1 | Notary Public | Speaker & Educator

Handing a ticket, a charge or a claim to someone you have never met is not a small thing. You want to know it is being handled carefully and correctly, by someone who understands what is at stake if it is not.

Butler Legal Services was founded by Sierra Walker, a paralegal licensed by the Law Society of Ontario since 2021, with more than 10 years in legal and client services across law firms, corporate governance and finance. Sierra is also a notary public, educator and mentor, and the founder of Notable Notary and The Notary Blueprint.

The same attention to detail and commitment to client service go into helping clients across Ontario, virtually and in person, with Small Claims Court, traffic and highway offences, and provincial offences.

Experience and credentials

  • Licensed Paralegal (LSO P1) since 2021. Licensed and regulated by the Law Society of Ontario.
  • 10+ years in legal and client services. Law firms, corporate governance, finance, auto finance and Small Claims.
  • Notary Public with a lifetime commission. Appointed by the Attorney General of Ontario.
  • Founder of Notable Notary (2021). Offering notary, apostille and translation services, online commissioning and more.
  • Founder of The Notary Blueprint (2023). An education and mentorship platform that has supported more than 5,000 notaries across Canada and the United States.
  • Speaker and educator. Founder and host of The Notary Blueprint Conference™, with more than 3,000 teaching hours. Has spoken for the Law Society of Ontario and for notary companies, conferences and events across Canada and the United States.
  • Recognition. National Notary Association Influencer of the Year finalist and Certified Apostille Processing Expert (CAPX™).
  • Community. Active member and former board member of the Ontario Association of Black Paralegals, and a participant in the documentary Judged Before The Judge (September 2026).

How Butler Legal Services works

  • Personalized legal strategy. Each matter gets a plan built around your goals and deadlines.
  • Transparent communication. Plain language and regular updates, with no surprises.
  • Client-focused commitment. Every matter is treated with the care it deserves.
  • Practical solutions. Honest advice on what is worth fighting, with the costs in view.
  • Clear limits. Only matters that can be properly handled within a paralegal’s scope are taken on.
