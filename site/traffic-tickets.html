Traffic ticket and highway offence paralegal in Ontario

Check your deadline first. Most tickets give about 15 days from the date on the ticket to respond. If that date has passed, reach out anyway. There may still be options.

Tickets and charges we handle

  • Speeding, stop sign and red light tickets
  • Distracted driving (hand-held device) and seat belt tickets
  • Careless driving and other serious Highway Traffic Act charges
  • Novice (G1 and G2) and commercial driver tickets
  • Driving without insurance, a licence or a valid permit

What a conviction can cost

The fine is only the start. A conviction can add demerit points, raise insurance premiums for years, and lead to licence consequences if points add up. Novice and commercial drivers face stricter rules. Serious charges, such as careless driving or stunt driving, can carry licence suspensions, vehicle impoundment and large fines.

Your options with a ticket

Your optionWhat it meansWorth knowing
Pay the ticketYou plead guilty and are convicted.Demerit points and insurance effects can follow. Get advice before paying if your record matters.
Meet the prosecutorAn early resolution meeting, where a reduced charge or penalty may be discussed.An offer is possible, never promised. Reviewing disclosure first shows what to ask for.
Ask for a trialThe prosecution must prove its case, and the evidence can be challenged.It takes longer and carries risk. Both are explained before you decide.

What the service includes

  1. Review of the ticket, the deadlines and what is at stake for your record.
  2. Request and review of the prosecution’s disclosure.
  3. A clear explanation of realistic options, including the risks of each.
  4. Negotiation with the prosecutor, or representation at trial.
  5. In many cases, an appearance on your behalf, so attending in person may not be needed.
No guarantees. A reduced charge or dismissal cannot be promised. What you do get is an honest view of what is likely, and whether fighting the ticket is worth it for you.

Prefer to handle the ticket yourself with expert support? Limited scope services include strategy advice, court coaching and single-appearance representation.
