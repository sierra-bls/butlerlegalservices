Speeding Tickets

Fight a speeding ticket in Ontario

Speeding tickets affect your demerit points, your insurance and, for some drivers, your licence. Find out your options before you pay.

Deadline first. Most speeding tickets give about 15 days from the date on the ticket to choose an option. Missing it can lead to a conviction without a hearing.

Speeding demerit points in Ontario

Speed over the limitDemerit points
1 to 15 km/h over0
16 to 29 km/h over3
30 to 49 km/h over4
50 km/h or more over (or 40 or more where the limit is under 80 km/h)Stunt driving charge, 6 points. See stunt driving

Fines are set by the number of kilometres over the limit, and they are increased in community safety zones and in construction zones when workers are present.

When it is worth getting help

  • The points would put you near a warning or suspension threshold
  • You are a novice (G1 or G2) driver, or you drive commercially
  • You drive for work, or a clean record matters for your insurance
  • You believe the reading, the signage or the stop was wrong

Your options with a speeding ticket

Your optionWhat it meansWorth knowing
Pay the ticketYou plead guilty and are convicted.Demerit points and insurance effects can follow. Get advice before paying if your record matters.
Meet the prosecutorAn early resolution meeting, where a reduced charge or penalty may be discussed.An offer is possible, never promised. Reviewing disclosure first shows what to ask for.
Ask for a trialThe prosecution must prove its case, and the evidence can be challenged.It takes longer and carries risk. Both are explained before you decide.

What the service includes

  1. Review of the ticket, deadlines and the points involved.
  2. A request for disclosure, including the officer’s notes and information about the speed measurement.
  3. A clear explanation of realistic options and the risk of each.
  4. Negotiation with the prosecutor, or representation at trial.
No guarantees. A reduced charge or dismissal cannot be promised. What you do get is an honest view of what is likely, and whether fighting is worth it for you.

Frequently asked questions

How many demerit points do I get for speeding in Ontario?

Speeding 1 to 15 km/h over the limit carries no demerit points. 16 to 29 km/h over carries 3 points, and 30 to 49 km/h over carries 4 points. Going 50 km/h or more over is treated as stunt driving, with 6 points and much heavier penalties.

How long do I have to respond to a speeding ticket?

Usually 15 days from the date on the ticket. The ticket shows the exact dates that apply. If the deadline has passed, reach out anyway, because there may still be options.

Will a speeding ticket raise my insurance?

A conviction can raise your premiums, sometimes for several years, depending on your insurer and how far over the limit you were. Resolving the ticket before you plead guilty can matter for your record and your rates.

Do speed camera tickets carry demerit points?

Automated speed camera tickets are generally issued to the vehicle owner and do not carry demerit points. Tickets issued by an officer do.

Do I have to attend court for a speeding ticket?

Often not. In many cases a licensed paralegal can appear on your behalf, though some hearings need you to attend, for example to give evidence. What applies to your ticket is confirmed up front.
